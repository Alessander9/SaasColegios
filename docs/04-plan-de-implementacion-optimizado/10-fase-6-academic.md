# 10. Fase 6 — Academic

Ahora:

```text
Academic
├── Curriculum
├── Courses
├── Teacher Assignment
├── Schedules
├── Evaluations
├── Grades
├── Attendance
├── Periods
├── Report Cards
└── Academic Records
```

Y tendremos reglas como:

```text
Grade Period
    ↓
OPEN
    ↓
Teacher enters grades
    ↓
SUBMIT
    ↓
Review
    ↓
PUBLISH
    ↓
LOCK
```

Esto es mucho mejor que simplemente tener un CRUD de notas.
